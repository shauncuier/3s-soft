## Section order they share

1. Split or centred hero: one headline (2 to 3 lines), one sentence of support, one primary CTA (nearly always a free audit or call) and at most one secondary.
2. Proof directly under the hero: logo strip, partner badges or a stats row.
3. Services grid (3 to 6 cards, icon + title + one line).
4. Case studies with one big number each.
5. Ratings row (Google, Clutch, Trustpilot stars + counts) and named testimonials.
6. Three or four step process.
7. FAQ accordion.
8. Closing CTA band, often dark.

## What makes them read as professional

- Calm motion: no floating elements, no auto-typing. Only Envision and Canopy move anything (a logo or photo slider).
- One accent colour on white, with one dark band for contrast.
- Headlines around 56 to 72 px on desktop, not poster-sized.
- Real imagery: product photos, dashboards, the founder. Never abstract art in the hero.
- Proof is specific and sourced (counts next to platform names).
- The same header, hero pattern and closing CTA on every page.

## What 3s-Soft takes from this (and what it can't)

| Pattern | 3s-Soft version |
|---|---|
| Free audit as primary CTA | Already the offer: free 5-product listing audit |
| Badge pill above headline (BBE) | "Amazon SPN provider · A+ Content & Cataloguing" pill |
| Split hero with real visual (Canopy, MAG) | Left copy, right a framed real case-study screenshot (Ivan Troy Brand Story) |
| Ratings row (Canopy, MAG) | Fiverr 5.0 (465), Upwork Top Rated 100% JSS, Amazon SPN, since 2015, each linked to its source |
| Case studies with numbers | Real facts only (variation counts, listings live). No revenue claims we can't source |
| Named testimonials | Verbatim Fiverr/Upwork reviews, static 3-column grid |
| Dark closing band | Existing charcoal CTA panel, arcs removed |
| Client logo strip | Not used: no written permission to show client logos yet |
| Big revenue stats ($3.2B, 5000+ brands) | Not used: we only show numbers with a public source |

## Decisions for the redesign

- Remove: floating tiles, logo arcs, typing search pill, scrolling ticker, auto-scrolling work wall and review rows, handwritten note, motion pause control.
- Keep: before/after listing toggle (useful proof, user-driven), proof bar, case cards, FAQ, dark closing panel.
- Motion: one short fade-up on hero text, nothing that loops.
- Every inner page uses one `PageHero`: breadcrumbs, small eyebrow, left-aligned H1 (40 to 60 px), intro, CTAs, optional right column for an image or form.
