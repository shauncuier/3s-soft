# SEO audit: 3s-soft.com (2026-11-24)

- **Type:** full site audit, live site.
- **Method:**
  - Crawled all 30 URLs in `sitemap.xml` and checked every internal link.
  - Ran Lighthouse 13.6 (mobile) on 4 pages.
  - Checked the Google index with a `site:` search.
  - Read the US results for 3 money keywords.
- **Limits:**
  - No Ahrefs or Search Console data, so there are no search volumes, rankings or backlink counts. Difficulty is estimated from who ranks.
  - The PageSpeed Insights API quota was used up, so field (CrUX) data is missing.

## Executive summary

The technical foundation is strong:
- All 30 pages return 200, with unique titles and descriptions, one H1 each, and self-referencing canonicals.
- There are no broken internal links, old URLs redirect, and HTTPS and the non-www host are enforced.
- Lighthouse scores SEO 100 / Accessibility 100 / Best Practices 100, and LCP is 1.0–1.6 s.

The biggest problem is **Google's index, not the site**. Google still shows the old site:
- Old titles for `/contact/`, `/about-us/`, `/services/lead-generation/` and `/services/ecommerce-product-listing/`.
- The fake case studies (RetailMax "150% sales growth", the MediCare HIPAA portal, EduTech, DataDriven).
- Old web-dev pages (`/services/web-development-mern/`, `/services/wordpress-customization/`, `/services/graphic-design/`).

All of these now 301 on the live site, but Google hasn't recrawled them.

**Top 3 priorities:**
1. Get Google to recrawl, using Search Console: sitemap, URL inspection, and a temporary sitemap of old URLs.
2. Build authority. There is no visible backlink profile, and the money keywords are held by established agencies, directories and listicles.
3. Make the content deeper: give each marketplace its own page, and grow blog posts from about 350 words to 1,000+.

**Overall:** strong foundation, needs work on indexing and authority.
